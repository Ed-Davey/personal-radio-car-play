# Personal Radio V1 feasibility study

**17 November 2026 · Decision: test the private pilot first.** Imported DRM-free tracks, a personal-use hourly podcast and DfT roadworks data give the pilot a path that avoids waiting for streaming and broadcast agreements. The provider-based V1 build remains gated by music rights, news clearance and timing, Cornwall incident coverage, and real iPhone/CarPlay proof. The [shaped pilot](Personal-Radio-Private-Pilot-Shaping-Plan.md) records the proposed scope changes and the conditions for proceeding.

The study follows the [Personal Radio V1 plan](https://chatgpt.com/library?file_id=libfile_5eb2fa8e5e608191b2d432bd7d07b095&account_id=personal). The target is one UK listener, one iPhone and CarPlay-equipped car, one playlist from the listener’s own music account, one synthetic voice, one hourly news source, and independently selected regional or general traffic. The listener starts the session before driving and it continues with the phone locked or another app in the foreground. Reputable alternatives to BBC news and travel are acceptable. Sky News Radio via IRN is the first news candidate to assess. Cornwall is the initial traffic test region, including B roads, village streets and unclassified lanes.

## Music, speech and CarPlay

**Spotify.** Development Mode is sufficient in audience size for a single Premium owner and one authorised user. For a new app, use a playlist the listener owns or collaborates on when checking item access. App Remote controls the Spotify app and reports player state. Spotify recommends disconnecting App Remote when the controlling app becomes inactive, and waking or resuming a disconnected player can require an app switch. The Web API can control an active Premium device, but command ordering across Player endpoints is unguaranteed. Neither path documents an atomic way to finish a song, prevent the next one from starting and resume it autonomously while locked. Shuffle, crossfade, external skips and queue changes can invalidate a prepared intro. Polling the duration and trying to pause on time would risk a clipped song or a wrong introduction. [Spotify quota modes](https://developer.spotify.com/documentation/web-api/concepts/quota-modes), [February 2026 API changes](https://developer.spotify.com/documentation/web-api/tutorials/february-2026-migration-guide), [App Remote lifecycle](https://developer.spotify.com/documentation/ios/concepts/application-lifecycle), [Start/Resume Playback](https://developer.spotify.com/documentation/web-api/reference/start-a-users-playback)

Spotify Developer Policy §III.5 restricts integration with other services’ content, §III.7 covers segueing and mixing audio, and §III.14 restricts ingesting Spotify content into AI models. Spotify’s definition of content includes metadata. The user’s own subscription and a gap between music and speech do not resolve those clauses. The written question should cover both external bulletins and reading a Spotify-provided title and artist through the proposed on-device speech engine. Ordinary authorised player-control tests can run separately from the combined experience. [Spotify Developer Policy](https://developer.spotify.com/policy), [Spotify Developer Terms](https://developer.spotify.com/terms)

**Apple Music.** `ApplicationMusicPlayer` gives the app its own queue and supports background playback of the current item. The most informative device probe is an app-owned playlist index and a single MusicKit item queued at a time. Confirm a natural completion while locked before playing speech. Then start the exact item announced. Test the differentiation between natural completion, a user pause, an interruption and a playback failure. Measure buffering and which player owns Now Playing and receives skip during speech. Apple’s MusicKit terms in §3.3.6(D) restrict synchronising MusicKit content with other content, require user-initiated playback and standard controls, and tie metadata to playback or playlist management. App Review §4.5.2 flags integrations that can require additional rights. Ask about the precise non-overlapping sequence and title/artist speech before selecting this provider. Review guidance and CarPlay entitlement approval are separate from MusicKit permissions. [ApplicationMusicPlayer](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/musickit/applicationmusicplayer), [Apple Developer agreement](https://developer.apple.com/support/terms/apple-developer-program-license-agreement/), [App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#apple-services)

**Presenter and car controls.** Use `AVSpeechSynthesizer` with one installed UK English voice and short factual templates, initially with independently permitted text. Target two to five seconds for most introductions. Resolve and recheck the track ID before speech and before starting playback. Record a missed intro if speech fails or the item cannot be verified. Test voices with the phone offline, relative volume, pronunciation and audio-session handoff. Configure iOS audio background mode and use CarPlay audio templates with accurate Now Playing and play/pause/skip controls. Obtain `com.apple.developer.carplay-audio` for the explicit App ID and use the matching provisioning profile. An Xcode-installed or ad hoc-signed build on the registered iPhone is a plausible private test route. Car speakers playing audio and the CarPlay simulator each give narrower evidence than the app’s own controls working on the target head unit. [Apple on-device speech synthesis](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/avfoundation/speech-synthesis), [Speech API](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/avfaudio/avspeechsynthesizer), [Background audio](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/avfoundation/configuring-your-app-for-media-playback), [CarPlay preparation](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/carplay/preparing-your-ios-app-for-carplay-development), [Music app sample](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/carplay/integrating-carplay-with-your-music-app), [Registered-device distribution](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/xcode/distributing-your-app-to-registered-devices)

## Playback contract and implementation

1. Start an active session before driving and resolve the next permitted track. On a natural track end, hold the next item. Play a fresh, due news introduction and complete bulletin, then relevant fresh traffic, then the verified song introduction and the corresponding full song. If a bulletin is late, continue music and try at the next eligible boundary. Play it once per scheduled hour and expire it at ten minutes past the hour under the plan’s defaults.
2. Poll traffic every two minutes where authorised. Filter using the manual local or general setting, with major events for general coverage. Queue new or materially changed incident IDs and expire pending breaking updates five minutes after the source update, or earlier if required by the source. Check the current effective window before announcing planned works. Limit spoken traffic to 90 seconds and between-hour insertions to one per five minutes. Drop duplicates, stale backlog and items belonging to an old source or region. Key hourly slots in UTC and present local times in `Europe/London` so the autumn clock change can distinguish the two local 01:00 slots.
3. Keep user pause separate from an internal speech hold. A deliberate pause or output change stops automatic progression. A skip in music resolves the next verified intro. A skip during an intro or bulletin goes to music without requeuing the skipped segment. Invalidate prepared speech when its track changes. While waiting for a source or recovering from a failure, show a clear state and avoid prompting for phone interaction while driving.

Use a native Swift/SwiftUI playback coordinator with separate music, bulletin and speech adapters. Drive it from playback and audio-session events during the active session. Recheck clocks and freshness on track completion. Apple schedules background refresh at its discretion, which makes it unsuitable for exact hourly triggers. Use official authorisation with minimum scopes, PKCE where required and Keychain storage. Keep region and playlist settings local, expire supplier caches and keep redacted diagnostics for the plan’s proposed seven days. A supplier credential that must stay private would require a small retrieval proxy. Street Manager live delivery would require a receiver for works notifications. A manual region and on-device speech avoid continuous location collection and sending track titles to a cloud voice provider. Validate accessibility on device with VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, labelled controls, and readable failure states. [Apple background strategies](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/backgroundtasks/choosing-background-strategies-for-your-app), [CarPlay design guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/carplay)

## Evidence from this study

- Reviewed the complete V1 plan, public platform contracts and integration documentation, supplier offers and data-feed licences. Verified public reports on local Cornwall roads, including a B-road and an unclassified road. Paid-feed coverage, live app delivery, supplier rights and CarPlay entitlement remain unverified.
- A local Python simulation passed **13 of 13 tests** using a synthetic clock, feed and queue. It covered delayed news at the first eligible boundary, one play per slot, expiry, news/traffic/song ordering, traffic polling and throttling, deduplication, material updates, outage staleness, source and region changes, pause, skip, changed track and the two UK clock-change slots. The 90-second fixture counted traffic clips only. Time the full spoken segment on device. The simulation measured scheduler decisions only.
- Local tooling check found Swift 6.2.3 and Command Line Tools selected. `xcodebuild -showsdks` could not run with that developer directory. No iOS or CarPlay build, device demo, or live supplier timing sample was produced. A macOS speech-file check with an installed UK voice and a default voice returned empty audio data in this environment, so voice quality and timing remain unmeasured.
